Help Center

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our jewelry, ordering, and care.

All our pieces are crafted with premium 316L Stainless Steel and finished with 18K Gold Plating. They are 100% tarnish-free, waterproof, and hypoallergenic—designed for everyday durability and luxurious style.

We offer a complimentary ring sizing guide that you can request through our website. Alternatively, you can visit any of our boutiques for a professional sizing. We also provide complimentary resizing within the first 6 months of purchase.

We offer complimentary insured shipping on all orders. Delivery typically takes 3-5 business days. We provide a 30-day return policy for unworn items in their original packaging. Custom and engraved pieces are final sale.

Yes, every piece comes with a lifetime warranty covering manufacturing defects. This includes prong tightening, re-polishing, and rhodium plating. We also offer annual complimentary cleaning and inspection services at all our boutiques.

Absolutely. Our bespoke design service allows you to create a one-of-a-kind piece. Schedule a consultation with our master jewelers who will guide you through the design process, from initial sketch to the final creation.

Store your jewelry separately to prevent scratching. Clean gently with a soft brush and mild soap solution. Avoid exposure to chemicals, perfumes, and extreme temperatures. We recommend professional cleaning every 6-12 months, which we provide complimentary for all our clients.
